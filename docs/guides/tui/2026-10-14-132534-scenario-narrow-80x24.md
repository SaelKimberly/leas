## Purpose and scope

Measures what the app does at the two sizes a small terminal actually is: which columns survive 80
columns, whether the hint rows survive, and what the layout does at 19 rows.

Does not cover: resize *during* a session (both sizes are launched fresh), colour, or the mouse.

## Corrections to expectations

- **E4/E5: the 80x24 status bar is row 23, and the panel starts at row 15.** The layout at height `h`
  when not compact is `1` (tabs) + `h-10` (content) + `8` (panel) + `1` (status), so at 24 rows the
  content is rows 1–14, the panel 15–22 and the status bar 23. My prediction of rows 13/20/21 came from
  an ad-hoc reading of the same screen that was off by two rows (probe8, collected in the
  [ad-hoc evidence](2026-10-14-132534-review-evidence-adhoc.md)); the transcript's region at step 7 is authoritative.
- **E8/E10: at 19 rows the app is compact, not in overlay mode.** `ui/mod.rs:78` sets
  `actions_compact = terminal height < 20` once at startup, so a fresh launch at 80x19 draws the table
  *and* a one-line actions bar — there is no state in which a fresh small launch loses the table. The
  overlay branch (`ui/mod.rs:964-969`, full panel replacing the content area) is only reachable when
  `actions_compact` is false *and* the height is under 20 — i.e. after `F1` toggles it, or after a
  resize during a session. `tiny-80x14` witnesses that state.
- **E9: the truncated status bar is real, and it is the last row.** Confirmed at step 19; the
  `content area replaced by the panel` half of the expectation was wrong for the reason above.

## Tooling friction

Same harness limits as `profiles-import-and-table` (pyte wide-char crash patched in the runner; wide
chars collapse one cell; buffer reads are text-only; no liveness check). Additional ones:

- **A wrong row index is invisible until the read comes back empty.** Three expectations here were
  off by one or two rows (E4, E5, E9) because the layout's row split depends on the terminal height;
  the empty `Line 21` at step 6 is the symptom to watch for.
- **`dimensions` is applied at launch only.** There is no resize step in the vocabulary, so the
  *resize* path that reaches overlay mode (`actions_compact` false, then a shrink) cannot be driven by
  a scenario; `F1` is the only way to observe the overlay at a small size in a headless run.
- **An `expect` on table content is unreliable at sizes where the table can be absent.** At 80x19 the
  `expect('Address')` in the ad-hoc probe matched; had the overlay branch been active it would have
  timed out instead, which is why this document's last step is a `choice` rather than an `assert`.

## Review of findings

At 80 columns the app keeps working but hides its verdicts: the columns that answer "does this
endpoint work?" are the ones dropped, and the hint row loses its tail. Both are fixable by priority
ordering rather than by shrinking anything.

**1. 80 columns silently drops the Test / Outbound / Country columns (major).** Evidence: step 4 —
`│   #           Address                             Feat    Protocol Info     █│` and step 5 —
`│        1 [🏴   203.0.113.77:443                 ][    ]=>{   vless/tcp/reali█│`. Why it matters:
`Test` is the column the profile list exists to show (delay, or the failure marker `[name]`), and it
is the first thing to disappear; the last surviving cell is also cut mid-token, so `reali█` reads like
a value. Recommendation: keep `Test` at every width, drop `Feat`/`Country`/`Outbound` in that order,
and ellipsize.

**2. The status-bar hint tail is cut, losing quit (major).** Evidence: steps 7 and 19 —
` Disconnected [→] Expand  [↑↓] Variant  [Ctrl+Enter/Ctrl+G] Connect  [Tab] Next`. Why it matters: the
hints are ordered by editor convenience, not user need, so `[?] Help` and `[q/Ctrl+C] Quit` — the two a
lost user wants — are exactly what falls off an 80-column screen. Recommendation: anchor the
right-hand hints and drop from the left, or make the hint set width-aware.

**3. A third of a 24-row terminal is an idle panel (minor).** Evidence: step 7 —
` ⚙ Actions Log  ////…`, `○ Disconnected`, `🖥 …`, `⏱ …`, `📊 …`, `📋 …` occupying rows 15–22 (of 24). Why
it matters: at the common 80x24 default the user loses 8 rows to a block whose content is one log line
and a restatement of row 23; the app already ships the right answer for this (the compact bar) but
gates it at 20 rows, below the common default. Recommendation: move the compact threshold up (e.g.
`height < 30`) or make `F1`'s state persist in the config.

**4. Two container idioms on adjacent tabs (nit).** Evidence: step 14 — ` Native Activity  ////…`
against step 9's `┌ Settings ─…┐` / `┌ Select a section ─…┐` box-drawing. Why it matters: the tab strip
is one surface; two border languages make the app look assembled from two codebases. Recommendation:
one container widget style (the box-drawing one the Logs/Profiles/Settings panes use).

### Verified good

- All six tabs fit the 80-column tab strip with their spacing intact (`Line 0`, step 3) and the search
  placeholder still fits on row 1 (step 18's region).
- The table keeps every column to the left of `Protocol Info` intact at 80 columns, including the
  `#` / bracket / flag gutter (steps 4–5), and the two-pane Settings layout adapts by shrinking the
  tree to 24 cells rather than overlapping (steps 9–10).
- Endpoint grouping survives the narrow width: the multi-variant endpoint keeps its `▶` marker at
  80 columns (step 5's region row 5).
- Below 20 rows the app picks the right compromise without being asked — the table stays and the
  panel collapses to one line (step 18), which is the better of the two layouts in this document even
  though the expectation had it backwards.
